# 📡 Sensor Photos & Videos Directory Guide

Place your sensor photos and video demonstrations in this directory:
`public/media/sensors/`

Because this folder is inside `public/`, files are served directly at `/media/sensors/<filename>`.

## 🎥 Formats & Optimization Tips

- **Photos**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` (Recommended 1200x800px or standard 4:3 / 16:9 phone camera photos).
- **Videos**: `.mp4` or `.webm` with H.264 video codec. Keep clips under 30 seconds for quick web loading.
- **In-App Upload Preview**: You can also use the interactive **"Upload / Preview Media"** button right inside any sensor card in the website UI to instantly test and preview photos or videos directly from your phone or computer!
